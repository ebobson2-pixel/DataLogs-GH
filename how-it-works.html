The flow

Three short steps. Then you're online.

DataLogs GH is built so you never hunt through a cart. The package is the start of checkout.

1

Tap a package

Pick MTN, AirtelTigo or Telecel and the GB you need. Popular and best-value packs are marked for you.

2

Enter the number

A popup asks for the recipient. We check the prefix so an MTN bundle doesn't try to land on a Telecel line.

3

Pay, then we send

Choose Mobile Money or card. Your order is saved live in DataLogs and queued for network delivery.

Good to know

Live orders, fast delivery.
