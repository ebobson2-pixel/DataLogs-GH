Markup Explanation (Remember to click save after applying markup)

Markup changes all your selling prices for the selected network based on the percentage you want. Markup is applied to the Base Price (your cost). For example, if Base Price = GH₵ 4.10, +10% gives GH₵ 4.51. After applying, you must click Save Prices to keep the changes. The markup affects only the currently selected network (MTN).

Your profit = Selling Price − Base Price. Use markup to increase all prices by a % (based on base price).

Recruit resellers

Turn this on if you want others to register under you as subagents. You set their buy/cost prices (like admin does for you). When they sell, you earn your markup and they earn theirs.

Example: your base is GH₵ 4.50, you charge the subagent GH₵ 5.00, they sell at GH₵ 5.50 → you get GH₵ 0.50, they get GH₵ 0.50.

You are a subagent

You registered under another agent. Set your store selling prices under Store pricing. Your cost prices come from your parent agent. You cannot recruit further resellers.

Prices for your subagents

Set the cost your subagents pay (their base). Must be at or above your own base. Your earn = their cost − your base.
